2026 CMS Think-Tank Summit
January 16-18, 2026
Moores School of Music at The University of Houston
Houston, TX
Ideas into Action:
Reimagining Music Schools for 2026 and Beyond
Program Support
CMS thanks our Summit sponsors whose support makes this gathering possible and impactful.
Dear CMS Members,
The College Music Society will host the 2026 Think-Tank Summit, scheduled for January 16–18 at the University of Houston’s Moores School of Music. The Summit theme, “Ideas into Action: Reimagining Music Schools for 2026 and Beyond,” will invite participants to join their CMS colleagues to discuss how educators and administrators can focus on advancing career-focused curricula, fostering innovation, and supporting diverse pathways for today’s music students. Conversations will be centered around the four pillars of Belonging, Creativity, Technology, and Advocacy:
Belonging
How can we sustain cultures of belonging regardless of upbringing and background?
Does all music matter, and if so, how do we represent this value in our curricula?
How can we reimagine our programming to serve music for all?
Creativity
How can we continue to amplify creativity in all that we do?
What does it mean to balance technique with intention?
How can we empower our students to sustain multifaceted careers in a rapidly changing creative and economic landscape?
Technology
What is the role of technology and AI in the creative process?
How can we prepare our students for the coming era of AI?
What lessons from the pandemic ought we better integrate moving forward?
Advocacy
How can we collectively advocate for the value of the arts in society?
What does it mean to empower and encourage our students and community to engage in citizen artistry and socially inspired art?
How can we make the case for music in higher education to government and society?
Following this weekend of action-oriented, expert-facilitated discussion and conversations, as well as peer-to-peer networking, participants of the 2026 CMS Think-Tank Summit will serve as active contributors to a guiding document outlining recommendations and strategies for implementing modern, career-focused programs across the academy and beyond.

Sixty-Ninth National Conference (2026)
November 12 - November 14, 2026
Amway Grand Plaza
Grand Rapids, Michigan
Source: Iceportal Content
Adaptive Advocacy: Collegiate Music Training at a Crossroads
Dear CMS Members,
The 2026 CMS Program Committee is excited to announce calls for participation in the 69th CMS National Conference held at the Grand Rapids Amway Grand Plaza hotel in Grand Rapids, MI, from November 12 - 14, 2026. The conference will be held in conjunction with the Association for Technology in Music Instruction (ATMI), National Association for Collegiate Wind and Percussion Instructors (NACWPI), and Pi Kappa Lambda (PKL). On behalf of the 2026 CMS National Conference Program Committee, I invite you to submit a proposal for participation.
The 2026 CMS National Conference Theme:
Adaptive Advocacy: Collegiate Music Training at a Crossroads
The world of collegiate music education is at a crossroads. Institutions of higher education face an array of challenges across technological, political, social, and economic paradigms, wherein creatives are poised to, together, imagine new models for sustainable music-making. The present moment asks each of us to envision what a vibrant future of music education, creation, performance, and scholarship might look like, in conversation with community-based action, coalition-building, advocacy, and innovative practice across all those realms. In line with the 2024-2025 Common Topic, we invite submissions that highlight current creative work in the following areas:
Performances and lecture recitals that imagine new ways of understanding the music of the past and the present, with forward-thinking considerations on music-making in the coming decade
Professional activities that illustrate the capacity for artistic expression to intersect with social action through collaboration and coalition-building
Creative research projects that explore innovations in the field of music education, performance, scholarship, and composition, through the lens of advocacy and social justice
Explorations of emergent technologies that pose both opportunities and potential challenges to music-makers and educators
The Program Committee invites participation from across all music disciplines, both those affiliated with performing arts institutions and companies and independent scholars, composers, performers, and educators. This year, the Committee is also excited to announce two special initiatives. For the first time, the 2026 conference will include a special call for student poster submissions, as well as featured presentations by CMS Regional Chapter Student Award recipients. More information about all proposal submission formats, as well as the full conference description, can be found by clicking on the “Calls for Program Participation” link. All proposals are due no later than 11:59pm Mountain Time on Monday, January 5, 2026.
Please join us in Grand Rapids, Michigan, a vibrant community filled with opportunities to engage with culture, art-making, stunning natural beauty, a world-class local craft food and beverage scene, and, of course, music. Situated in the heart of the Midwest, it is the perfect place to come together, share, learn from one another, and imagine the potentialities of this crossroads moment. We can’t wait to see you there!
Sincerely,
Jonathan Kuuskoski
2026 CMS National Conference Program Committee Chair

Books and Monographs
Cultural Expressions in Music
Created in 2009, Cultural Expressions in Music is a series of referred monographs which seeks to promote and share the diversity of perspectives, cultures, experiences, philosophies, and contributions of The College Music Society’s membership. The series is now entering a new phase. The Editorial Board is soliciting contributions that address music as narrative and musicians as narrators of the migratory movement of people, cultures and civilizations through time and space, focusing on musical genres, styles, repertoire, and practices. Please see the Call for Proposals in the left menu.
The College Music Society website, an important tool to enhance CMS scholarship, is available for dynamic audio, video, and other multi-media documents to accompany monographs. The length of the book is open and flexible depending on the content of the monograph.
Submission target dates are January 15 and December 15, but proposal submission and inquiries are welcome at any time.
