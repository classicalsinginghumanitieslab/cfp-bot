96th Meeting of the Music Library Association, March 3-6, 2027
2027 Annual Meeting
The 96th Annual Meeting of the Music Library Association will be held at the Westin Book Cadillac Hotel in Detroit, Michigan, March 3 – 6, 2027. This will be a fully in-person experience, and we will not be offering a virtual-streaming option for program sessions.
Detroit provides an accessible, vibrant, and culturally rich urban hub for members traveling from across the country. As the birthplace of Motown, techno, and monumental jazz and rock legacies, the city offers attendees an inspiring backdrop steeped in recorded and performed history. Join us to connect with regional archives, local collections, and fellow performing arts specialists!

About MLA
The Music Library Association is the professional association for music libraries and librarianship in the United States. Founded in 1931, it has an international membership of librarians, musicians, scholars, educators, and members of the book and
music trades. Complementing the Association’s national and international activities are ten regional chapters that carry out its programs on the local level.

2026 Meeting in Salt Lake City – MLA Conferences
96th Meeting of the Music Library Association, March 3-6, 2027
2026 Annual Meeting
The 95th Annual Meeting of the Music Library Association was held at the Hilton Salt Lake City Center in Salt Lake City, UT, from February 25-28, 2026, as a hybrid in-person and virtual meeting.
Committee, subcommittee, interest group, chapter, task force, and user group meetings took place online after the conference, in the week of March 9–13, 2026.
Session Videos
Session recordings can be viewed by the public on Vimeo.
Committee, subcommittee, interest group, chapter, task force, and user group meetings are recorded at the chair or coordinator’s discretion, and are available only to registered attendees on the Cvent Attendee Website.
The New Business Town Hall is available to registered attendees on the Cvent Attendee Website and by request from the Convention Managers.

Ways to Support Colleagues & the 96th Annual Meeting
MLA’s Development efforts are focusing on 3 areas that help to make our Annual Meetings so special: the General Conference Registration Fund, Dependent Care Vouchers, and support for the Big Band/Rock Band.
Our community is made stronger through a culture of care–supporting our members who need financial assistance to attend our annual meeting. And our annual meetings show why our community is so amazing: great colleagues, sharing our professional practices and scholarship with one another, and capping off our time together with the party atmosphere that only the Big Band and Rock Band (our own members!) can provide.
Why Your Support Matters
Every contribution, no matter the size, ripples through our community to fund vital initiatives:
Impact Area
How Your Donation Helps
Supporting Our Members
The GCRF Fund provides conference registration support for early-career and student members and those members who do not have professional development money. Dependent Care Vouchers help members who are parents and caregivers attend our meetings.
Strengthening the Association
When new and long-standing members can connect at our annual meeting, we grow and maintain those professional and personal connections that contribute to a stronger association.
Advancing the Fun
The Big Band and Rock Band performances are such an important part of our annual meeting atmosphere–such a joy-filled tradition.
How to Give
All supporters will be formally acknowledged for their generosity and dedication to our shared mission.

Refund Policy
MLA Conference Refund Policy for 2027 Meeting
All requests for change in registration or cancellation must be submitted in writing to the MLA Convention Managers at cm@musiclibraryassoc.org.
Refunds will be automated as follows:
Cancellation Date (Deadline)
Refund Amount

(75+ days before conference start)
100%

80%

(31-44 days before conference start)
50%
On or after Monday, February 1, 2027
(within 30 days of conference start)
0%
Refund Processing
Requested and approved standard refunds will be processed within 10-14 business days following the final cancellation deadline (March 6, 2027).
General Conditions & Non-Refundable Fees
Conference Fees Only: Refunds apply only to conference registration fees. Fees for special events, tours, workshops, MOUG meetings, or other add-ons are non-refundable, unless a separate refund policy is explicitly published for a particular offering.
No-Shows/Early Departure: No refunds will be given for failure to attend (No-Shows), for leaving the conference early, or for any unused portions of the event.
Non-Transferable: Registration fees cannot be transferred to another person, program, or to dues.
Travel and Funding: Registration fees are not refunded for weather, travel-related delays, cancellations, or visa issues (unless covered by the Extenuating Circumstances section below). Lack of travel funding is not a valid extenuating circumstance.
Recipient: Refunds are issued to the original registrant. If payment was made by an institution, the registrant is responsible for managing reimbursement with that entity.
Extenuating Circumstances (Full Refund Exception)
Written requests for a full refund outside the standard schedule may be submitted to the Convention Managers by Saturday, March 6, 2027. Full refunds may be considered for the following:
Serious Health/Family: Death or serious illness/debilitating health condition of the registrant, an immediate family member, or a significant other (domestic partner/spouse), which necessarily restricts travel and participation.
Public Health Restrictions: Inability to travel due to an official, enforced lockdown, isolation, or quarantine; institutional travel restrictions; or visa restrictions, all related to a public health emergency (e.g., pandemic, epidemic).
Other Extenuating Events: Other rare and severe circumstances (e.g., the death of a service animal on which a disabled Registrant’s attendance is predicated).
Please Note:
Documentation of the circumstance may be required.
Individuals receiving a full refund will not have access to the online program.
Meeting Changes or Cancellation
Change of Format: If circumstances outside of MLA’s control (e.g., acts of God, war, public health emergency, transportation disruption) make an in-person meeting inadvisable or impossible, the meeting may be converted to an entirely virtual format.
Full Notice and Instructions: If the meeting should be converted or cancelled, all those registered will receive notice and instructions on how MLA is proceeding with the event.
Post-Change Cancellation: All attendees are automatically registered for the new format. If a registrant cancels after the format change is announced, the Standard Refund Schedule still applies (see table).
Liability: If the meeting is canceled or postponed due to events beyond MLA’s reasonable control, MLA is not liable for any resulting damages, costs, or losses incurred by participants (e.g., accommodation, transportation, or additional orders).
Exhibitor Addendum
Exhibitor refunds will be subject to the standard policy, minus the cost of any expenses incurred by MLA specifically for the exhibitor (e.g., exhibit tables, special paraphernalia).
Questions regarding this policy must be submitted in writing to the MLA Convention Managers at cm@musiclibraryassoc.org.
