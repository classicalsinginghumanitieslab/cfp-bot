Community Choice Call for Proposals – MLA Conferences
96th Meeting of the Music Library Association, March 3-6, 2027
Community Choice Call for Proposals
Call for Community Choice Proposals & Second Call for Poster Proposals
The 96th Annual Meeting of the Music Library Association is scheduled to be held at the Westin Book Cadillac Hotel in Detroit, Michigan, March 3–6, 2027. This will be a fully in-person experience, and we will not be offering a virtual-streaming option for program sessions. The Program Committee invites proposals for the Community Choice session, as well as additional poster proposals, by 11:59pm PDT on Saturday, November 7, 2026.
We encourage proposals that are topically or practically relevant to libraries, collections, or librarianship in the wide scope of the performing arts: music, theatre, dance, performance studies, popular entertainment, motion pictures, and broadcasting, especially those addressing new or emerging areas. We welcome proposals from library, archives, and information technology professionals, students, faculty, and others, regardless of MLA membership. We especially encourage proposals from individuals and communities that have been historically underrepresented and/or marginalized in library work and in society as a whole.
SECOND CALL FOR POSTER PROPOSALS: if your poster proposal has already been accepted, you do not need to resubmit.
Proposal Types
Community Choice presentations are 25 or 55 minutes in length, and may be individual, joint, or panel. Poster presentations take place within the designated poster session, and may also be individual or joint.
Submission Guidelines
An abstract is required and should be no longer than 3000 characters (not words).
Provide the names and email addresses of all known presenters.
Any individual may participate as a presenter in up to two sessions total (individual, group, or poster). Serving as a session moderator and/or participating in scheduled Committee business meetings and Interest Group meetings do not count towards this two-session limit.
Those who submit proposals involving research conducted on living human subjects will be asked whether Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval was or will be sought. Answering “no” will not disqualify a proposal from consideration.
Sessions at the MLA Annual Meeting are for the educational benefit of attendees; proposals promoting or selling products or services will not be accepted.
Proposal Submission
Proposals must be submitted online via the Session Proposal Form.
This form will be open for submissions until 11:59pm PDT on November 7.
Selection Process for Community Choice
Titles and abstracts (but not proposer name and affiliation) will be shared with the MLA community via an online survey, for a two-week period. Following a vote by the MLA community, up to three proposals will be selected for our annual meeting program. An 85-minute time-slot for Community Choice has been reserved in the 2027 program.
Selection Process for Posters
Poster proposals are reviewed by members of the Program Committee. Proposals will be selected on the strength of their abstracts, based on these criteria:
The main points of your proposal
Relevance to attendees
How your proposal is unique or different from others that may address the same topic
Learning objectives, goals, or outcomes
Special consideration will be given to proposals
Submitted by first-time presenters
Pertaining to theatre libraries and librarianship
Addressing new or emerging areas of music, theatre, and/or performing arts librarianship
Fostering cross-disciplinary collaboration
Collection development
Archives, rare books, special collections
Cataloging/metadata
Copyright
The Program Committee will evaluate poster proposals as anonymous submissions except in cases where the identity of presenters is a vital part of the proposal. The Program Committee will additionally strive to select a balance of proposals reflecting the range of institutions (academic, public, special, etc.), specializations (cataloging/metadata, archives, information literacy, collection development, etc.), and audiences (librarians, archivists, paraprofessionals, students and emerging professionals, retirees, etc.) represented at our Meetings.
Please Note
While in-person will be the standard mode of presentation, Community Choice panels may include remote or recorded presentations, with the understanding that the in-person panelist(s) will be responsible for managing the technology for the remote or recorded presenter(s).
We look forward to your proposals! Please contact the Program Chair, Linda Fairtile (program@musiclibraryassoc.org), with any questions.

2026 Meeting in Salt Lake City – MLA Conferences
96th Meeting of the Music Library Association, March 3-6, 2027
2026 Annual Meeting
The 95th Annual Meeting of the Music Library Association was held at the Hilton Salt Lake City Center in Salt Lake City, UT, from February 25-28, 2026, as a hybrid in-person and virtual meeting.
Committee, subcommittee, interest group, chapter, task force, and user group meetings took place online after the conference, in the week of March 9–13, 2026.
Session Videos
Session recordings can be viewed by the public on Vimeo.
Committee, subcommittee, interest group, chapter, task force, and user group meetings are recorded at the chair or coordinator’s discretion, and are available only to registered attendees on the Cvent Attendee Website.
The New Business Town Hall is available to registered attendees on the Cvent Attendee Website and by request from the Convention Managers.
